## Walking is the most underrated fat loss tool

Walking does not get the respect it deserves. In a fitness culture obsessed with HIIT and six-day training splits, walking gets dismissed as something your grandmother does. That dismissal is costing you results.

The human body evolved to walk. For 99% of human history, people walked 15,000–20,000 steps per day not as exercise but as life. Our bodies are not just adapted to this — they are optimised for it.

Modern sedentary life has removed almost all of that movement. The average desk worker now takes fewer than 5,000 steps per day. This single change contributes directly to weight gain, cardiovascular decline, and poor metabolic health.

This guide gives you everything you need to fix it.

---

## What makes walking so effective for fat loss

At low intensities — 60–70% of your maximum heart rate — your body preferentially burns fat as its primary fuel source. This is called Zone 2. Brisk walking and incline walking naturally keep most people in Zone 2, making walking one of the most efficient fat-burning activities available.

Walking also has an advantage no other form of cardio can match: **zero recovery cost**. You can walk every single day, indefinitely, without the joint stress and fatigue that forces runners to take rest days. That means walking compounds — every day adds to the total.
