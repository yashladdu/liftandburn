## Why Torso & Limbs?

Most upper/lower splits train chest, back, shoulders, and arms all in one session. By the time you get to your biceps and triceps, they are already fatigued from pressing and rowing. The Torso & Limbs split fixes this.

Train your torso one day. Train your arms and legs the next. Arms come in completely fresh on Limbs day — before the legs, which are the most demanding muscle group. Everything gets trained with maximum quality, every session.

---

## The Philosophy

**Low volume, high intensity.** Two working sets per exercise. Every set is taken to 1 rep in reserve (RIR 1) — you stop when you could do exactly one more rep with good form. No junk volume. No sets done to fill time. Every set counts.

**Supersets save you time.** Many exercises in this program work opposing muscle groups — biceps vs triceps, chest vs back, quads vs hamstrings. Superset them. Each muscle rests while the other works. Sessions take under 1 hour.

**Progressive overload.** Add weight whenever you complete both working sets at the top of the rep range. Even 1.25 kg counts. Log every session.

**It never ends.** When you finish Week 12, start Week 1 again with your new working weights. This is the only program you need.
